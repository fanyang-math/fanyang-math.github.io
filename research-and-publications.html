In preparation

  1. Exponential mixing for harmonic measures of transversally conformal foliations (with S. Alvarez, R. Ures and J. Yang).
    For transversally conformal foliations without transverse holonomy-invariant measures, we establish exponential convergence of the leafwise heat diffusion towards the harmonic measure.

  2. A lower bound of entropy for singular hyperbolic chain recurrence classes (with M. Li, J. Yang and R. Zheng).
    In this article, we construct a new example of a robustly nontrivial chain recurrence class that is sectional-hyperbolic but not Lyapunov stable, and show that the example has positive topological entropy, contains periodic orbits, and is C^1 generically a robustly isolated homoclinic class.
    The main tool is a lower bound of the topological entropy of a sectional-hyperbolic chain recurrence class by the product of its sectional expansion rate with the lower box-counting dimension along the center-unstable normal direction.

  3. Exponential equidistribution and unique ergodicity for expanding foliations (with R. Saghin, R. Ures and J. Yang).
    In this paper we consider any perturbation of the time-one map of the geodesic flow on a compact surface with negative curvature. It has been established that either there exists a unique measure of maximal entropy (MME) which has zero center exponent, in which case the perturbed map is the time-one map of a topological flow, or there exist two MMEs with positive/negative center exponent, respectively.
    We show that in the second case, the unstable foliation has a unique transversal invariant measure (and consequently the horocycle flow is uniquely ergodic), and equidistributes exponentially to the MME with negative center exponent. The same results hold for the stable foliation which equidistributes to the MME with positive exponent.
    We propose two conjectures on the strong unstable foliation of Anosov diffeomorphisms on T^3: they are uniquely ergodic, and equidistribute exponentially to the measure of maximal uu-entropy.

  4. A countable partition for singular flows based on filtration structure: construction and application (with M. Li, J. Yang and R. Zheng).
    We construct a countable partition for flows with hyperbolic singularities such that its metric entropy for invariant probability measures is uniformly bounded. As an application, we show the upper semi-continuity of the entropy map for singular flows away from homoclinic tangencies. The construction is based on a filtration structure on the Grassmannian manifold and improves [10] above.

  5. Limiting return times distribution for arbitrary null sets until one encounters an obstacle (with N. Haydn).
    “About the exciting story of the intrepid random traveler and how many times he returned home before being swallowed by a black hole, forever vanishing from the face of the earth.”
    In this paper we study the number of entries to a neighborhood U (‘home’) of some measure zero set, before the orbit enters another open set V (a black hole). Assuming that the system has sufficient mixing property, we show that the number of entries converges in distribution to a compound Poisson distribution with an exponentially distributed stopping time.
